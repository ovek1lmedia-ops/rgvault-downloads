What is RGVault?

RGVault is a free, unofficial RedGifs app for Android built for watching short-form adult videos in a clean, full-screen reel feed. Instead of fighting a mobile browser, you get one vertical feed, a searchable directory of more than 1,800 niches, creator pages, and a private vault for videos you want to keep. It is an independent client — not affiliated with RedGifs — and it never asks you to create an account.

The app is designed around the way people actually use short-form video: swipe, watch, like, repeat. Explore lets you jump from Trending into categories such as Amateur, Asian, Ebony, Latina, Cosplay, Fitness and dozens more. Inside a niche you can sort videos by Trending, Most viewed, Newest, Oldest or Most liked, not just sort the niche list. When you find something worth keeping, save it to the offline vault or trim a six-second clip and export it as a GIF.

Features

Full-screen reels feed

One vertical pager for Home, niches, tags, search and creator pages. Prefetching and adaptive playback keep swiping smooth.

Explore 1,800+ niches

Browse the full niche directory by category, A–Z or popularity. Every niche opens as its own sorted video feed.

Search creators, tags & videos

Find a creator by name, search a tag, or look up a specific video. Results use the same player as the main feed.

Offline vault

Save videos to your phone and watch them without a connection. Vault, likes and history survive in-place app updates.

GIF maker & export

Drag A–B handles over any downloaded clip, preview the loop, and share a six-second GIF to any app.

Private by default

No sign-up, no cloud sync, no tracking SDK. History, likes and filter preferences stay on your device.

Data Saver

Force SD playback and pause prefetching on cellular data, so a scroll session does not eat your plan.

Limits & filters

Hide topics, hide AI-generated reels, block a creator, or set a daily screen-time limit — all reversible from one screen.

How to install the RedGifs APK

  1. Open the Download tab and tap the arm64 APK button.
  2. When Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. This is a one-time permission.
  3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install. If Play Protect warns about a sideloaded app, choose Install anyway after checking you downloaded it from this site or the official GitHub Releases page.
  4. Launch RGVault and start scrolling. To update later, install the new APK over the old app — never uninstall first, or your vault and history will be erased.

Tested on Android 7.0 and newer. arm64-v8a covers most phones from the last several years; armeabi-v7a is available for older 32-bit devices.

RedGifs app FAQ

Is RGVault an official RedGifs app?

No. RGVault is an independent, unofficial client for Android. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by RedGifs. Videos are hosted by RedGifs and belong to their creators; RGVault only provides a different way to browse and save them.

Is the app free?

Yes. RGVault is free to download and use. An occasional inline sponsored page keeps the lights on, and you can tip the developer from the Support tab if you want to.

Do I need a RedGifs account?

No account is required to browse reels, explore niches, save videos or export GIFs. Your vault, likes and history are stored locally on your phone.

Can I watch videos offline?

Yes. Anything you save to the vault is stored on your device and plays without a connection. Streaming the main feed still needs internet access.

Which APK should I download?

Choose arm64-v8a for almost every modern Android phone. If you have an older 32-bit device, use the armeabi-v7a build from GitHub Releases.

Will an update delete my saved videos?

No, as long as you install the new APK over the existing app. Uninstalling first removes the app's private storage, including the vault, likes and history.

Is this a RedGifs downloader for every video?

RGVault can save the videos it can play, including HD where the source provides it. It does not bypass paywalls, private accounts or creator restrictions. Please respect creators and RedGifs' terms.
